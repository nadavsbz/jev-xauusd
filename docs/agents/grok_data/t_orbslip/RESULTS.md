# Results (single preregistered run, no tuning)

58,873 trades, 30 large-cap US stock CFDs, 2018–2025, Dukascopy 1-minute bid/ask, mid prices unless noted. No relative-volume filter. Commission $0.0035/share/side is included in every net-R column except sensitivity B.

## Headline
Unfiltered 5-minute ORB on liquid names has a tiny positive gross mean of **+0.050 R** (win rate 16.3%, stop-out 83.3%, median R = −1). That is in the same ballpark as the Concretum paper’s +0.08 R for RelVol>1, and it is not enough to survive realistic costs:

| slippage $/share/side | mean cost (R, incl. comm) | mean net R | verdict |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.00 | 0.039 | +0.011 | barely positive |
| 0.01 | 0.151 | **−0.100** | cost-killed |
| 0.02 | 0.262 | **−0.212** | cost-killed |
| cross-spread (sens. B, no comm) | (CFD spread) | **−0.278** | cost-killed |

By R$ = 0.10×ATR14 bucket (preregistered “cost-killed if mean net R ≤ 0”):

- **R$ < $0.10** (17.6% of trades): already net-negative at $0 slip (−0.037); −0.36 R at 1¢; −0.68 R at 2¢.
- **$0.10–0.25** (27.3%): +0.008 at $0, **−0.119 at 1¢**, −0.246 at 2¢. Cost-killed at 1¢.
- **$0.25–0.50** (22.4%): already −0.016 at $0.
- **$0.50–1.00** (18.6%): survives $0 and barely 1¢ (+0.002), killed at 2¢ (−0.028).
- **R$ ≥ $1.00** (14.0%): still +0.073 at 2¢. This bucket is optimistic (see split caveat).

By year, only 2018 stays slightly positive after 1¢ (+0.023). 2019–2025 are all net-negative at 1¢. Gross itself is negative in 2022 and 2023.

This is **not** a verdict on the full “stocks in play” strategy. It answers only the exit-mechanism question: with R = 10% of ATR, 1–2¢ per share per side eats more R than the unfiltered (and likely the filtered) edge on most names.

## Caveats
1. Data are **Dukascopy CFD quotes**, not SIP equity prints. Quoted spreads are wider than the cash tape, so sensitivity B is a conservative worse-fill bound.
2. **Splits are not back-adjusted.** A day with |open/prev close| > 30% is dropped, but earlier dollar prices stay unadjusted (AMZN and GOOGL 20:1 in 2022, NVDA 4:1 and 10:1, AAPL 4:1, TSLA 5:1 and 3:1). Pre-split trades therefore have inflated R$ and understated cost-in-R. The low-R$ buckets (cheap names and post-split periods, ~45% of trades with R$ < $0.25) are the honest stress case, and they are already cost-killed at 1¢.
3. Low-priced CFDs have stale/flat minutes (T 25%, AAL 13%, PFE 9.5%). Those names also have tiny R$ and are the most cost-sensitive.
4. About 18–21 short/gappy days per name are dropped; almost no residual split-like days remain in the trade set. Coverage is ~1,960 traded days / name out of ~2,011 raw RTH days.
