# Darwinex public DARWIN screening (read-only)

**As of:** 11 Nov 2026. No account/sign-up/login, portfolio action, or investing was used.

## Findings and confidence

`https://www.darwinex.com/darwins` returned a 404; the public explorer at `https://www.darwinex.com/explore` worked. Its public ranking cards and public DARWIN pages were used. The proposed `https://www.darwinexzero.com/rankings` returned a not-found page. No cookie banner appeared, so there was nothing to dismiss.

This is a **provisional review list**, not five verified matches. Only LSP looks like a strong overall fit from the available data; EVH and LSI are low-frequency but have much lower returns; DAE and MQR meet the average-hold-duration screen but have high trade counts. The public pages do not expose the underlying symbol list, open-position count, losing-position duration, or enough Investable Attributes to rule out grid/martingale behavior. Their generic risk description says Darwinex's separate risk engine targets monthly VaR (95%) of 6.5%; this is not proof about the provider's strategy.

Returns below are public DARWIN/index history, gross before fees—not a verified underlying-account return. CAGR is an approximate compounding of the public lifetime total from the stated DARWIN investable date to 11 Nov 2026. Monthly-sampled drawdown is calculated from the public monthly-return table and can understate true intramonth maximum drawdown. Darwin profile headline metrics were internally inconsistent with the dated return table for several entries; those headline DD/track-record fields are not treated as reliable. All require further diligence.

### Screening notes

- Trade counts/month are rough averages calculated from the public total trade count and elapsed DARWIN track-record months.
- A public monthly-return-table drawdown is a month-end estimate, **not** a substitute for a published daily/intraday max drawdown. A value under 25% here does not certify the user's max-DD limit.
- Darwinex's investor-facing pages describe its risk engine and investor VaR control, but do not show the requested underlying losing-position duration/open-position-count/Investable Attributes for these profiles. No strategy is labeled “not grid/martingale” based on the evidence available.
- The “main instruments” are not given as symbols on the pages reviewed; only the broad CFDs asset classes above were visible.
- Excluded example: **SGP** had 3,124 trades and 5d18h45m average duration, but its public monthly table gives a **34.81% month-end drawdown estimate**, exceeding the requested limit. High-return names such as THA were also not shortlisted where the public average duration was short (THA: 2h54m, 18,826 trades).

## Bottom line

If strict adherence is required, **LSP is the only clear first-pass match** among the public candidates inspected. EVH and LSI are the better low-frequency alternatives, but return targets are not met. DAE/MQR are exploratory only due trade volume. Confirm full drawdown and underlying strategy style from deeper public disclosures before treating any as a fit.
