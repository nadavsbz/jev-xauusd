Three Weeks Without a Trade — And That’s Okay

It’s been three weeks since I launched Personal Edge, and during that time I haven’t taken a single trade on my personal account.

I wanted to briefly explain why.
I don’t have a goal of forcing a trade every day, every week. My goal is to enter only when there is a clear edge and the key conditions align.

Over these three weeks, the market approached several interesting areas, but at the actual entry points, I didn’t have enough confluence to justify the risk.

In one situation, my limit order was triggered on only some of my accounts. On the others, price didn’t reach the entry and reversed immediately. I didn’t chase the move afterwards.

For me, the principle is simple:
No edge → No trade.

If the market gives me a high-quality setup, I’ll take it. If it doesn’t, I’ll wait.
Trading isn’t a competition to make the most trades.
The goal is to be there when the risk is actually worth taking.

Let’s keep watching↗️

One Good Trade✔️

One trade on the S&P 500 was enough to recover last month's drawdown and bring the account back to breakeven.
At the same time, I've made an adjustment to my trading strategy. From now on, I'll be focusing exclusively on the S&P 500.

The main driver behind the currency market is the US dollar, which adds another layer of complexity when trading EUR/USD. While I'm confident in my ability to read the DXY, managing the relationship between the dollar and the euro introduces additional variables.
I believe simplicity leads to greater efficiency. By focusing on one instrument, I can concentrate on what matters most: market structure, high-quality setups, and execution.

One market. Clearer scenarios. Better focus.
Let's see whether a simpler approach leads to better results.
